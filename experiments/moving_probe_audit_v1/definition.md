# Moving probe audit v1 — exploratory course accounting

The moving-only timing model study is closed NO-GO. Its three moving probe
accuracy deltas are already known to be negative. Freeze this unblinded
diagnostic before inspecting any per-course, timing-block or truth-side
prediction breakdown. Use its six saved exports and fixed exam metadata;
generate no data, fit no model and run no inference.

Verify the full executed sample index/labels and the complete geometric probe
against the unchanged selectors, plus every export's seed, receipt, exam
identity and original aggregate readings. Keep the original strict probe
definition and all frames. Verify the original study's saved source/input
hashes and nine protected artifacts. The new module is additive; do not
weaken or rewrite the closed study's frozen Python inventory or manifest.

For every seed 0/1/2, transit world classic/dense/moving and exam block
approach/immediate/all, retain all 27 cell summaries. Also reconstruct each
seed's original all-world aggregate. Each cell reports:

- Frames and distinct course IDs, safer-left/right truth support, and each
  model's correct counts and accuracy by truth side. Missing classes have
  null rates, not chance scores. Give the two constant-direction reference
  accuracies from truth prevalence; these are descriptive, not new models.
- The complete paired correctness table: both correct, control-only correct,
  candidate-only correct, both incorrect. Count frames and distinct courses
  in every entry. Their net change divided by all cell frames must reproduce
  the original delta; no frame is removed or reweighted for this check.
- Every contributing course, with frame count, timing/world identity,
  correct counts, accuracy delta and its contribution to the full-world
  frame-weighted delta. Sum contributions back to that original delta.
  Retain counts of improving/declining/unchanged courses and the equal-course
  mean delta as an explicitly different descriptive estimand. Do not use
  this alternative weighting to rescue the original gate.

The saved export contains only `veer_correct` and `veer_gt_left`, not the
left/right scores or their gap. The scorer uses strict inequalities, so a
false correctness flag can mean a wrong strict ordering OR an exact tie.
Do not infer a chosen opposite side, a tie count, a probability margin or
calibration from that flag. Report identified bounds on strict left/right
preference counts: correct examples identify the true side; incorrect
examples admit the opposite side or a tie. Tie-count bounds are zero to
the number incorrect. Bounds are marginal and cannot be combined as if
every extreme were simultaneously attainable. Recovering exact margins or
ties would require separately authorized/prospectively specified inference.

Reconcile the twelve original all/world metrics and course contributions
to absolute tolerance 1e-12. These are accounting checks, not scientific
performance gates. Empty cells retain zero counts and null rates. Report
every seed and both blocks, with no selected course subset, new bin,
bootstrap, confidence claim, threshold change, comparison to other exams,
training-exposure/gradient attribution or flight certification.

This audit can locate the loss and expose limits of the saved instrument;
it cannot identify a training mechanism. All earlier NO-GOs, recipe defaults
and champion artifacts remain unchanged. No outcome releases another fit.
